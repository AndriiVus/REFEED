Who it is for

Two sides of the same problem

ReFeed+ addresses both ends of the food waste chain: the businesses discarding it and the people who need it.

Primary

Local Food Businesses

Grocery stores, bakeries & cafes

  • Paying up to €500 per month in waste disposal fees
  • Facing growing sustainability pressures
  • Producing soon to expire or unsold food daily
  • Seeking low effort community reputation gains

Core Need: A cost effective solution that reduces disposal costs and helps the community.

Secondary

Underprivileged Individuals

Adults experiencing housing insecurity

  • Many lack smartphones or data access
  • Rely on physical locations for services
  • Shelters and food banks cannot meet demand
  • Search for accessible food sources

Core Need: Consistent access to healthy food without needing an app or phone.
