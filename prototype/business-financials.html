Business strategy

Social value with a business case

REFEED+ connects community impact with a practical, cost-saving reason for Irish businesses to participate.

Start small to validate the in-store workflow and ensure POS integration is frictionless for staff.

Pilot

Run a controlled pilot in a few stores to validate the process, train staff and confirm POS workflows are simple and reliable.

Expand through local business clusters, turning many stores into micro-distribution points.

Scale

Grow by onboarding neighbouring stores and partners so each cluster provides consistent local access for beneficiaries.

Value proposition

Waste reduction is the strongest economic argument for Irish businesses — divert surplus food, lower local disposal fees (often up to €500/mo), and improve sustainability reputation across Ireland.

Revenue model — diversified & low-friction

  • Core subscriptions (SaaS): Standard tier €20/month for food businesses. Add-ons (Pro) for larger chains: advanced reporting, volunteer routing, priority support.
  • Data-as-a-Service (DaaS): Tiered licensing (e.g., Irish universities €60/mo; NGO/Gov €300+/mo) for anonymised, real-time neighbourhood insights useful to local councils and researchers.
  • Partnerships & grants: Pilot funding, Irish government and local authority schemes (for example the Local Enhancement Programme), and research partnerships to reduce early-stage runway risk.

Financial milestones (Year 1)

Goal: multiply income ~2x every 4 months as we move from launch to city-wide maturity across Irish urban areas.

REFEED+ is targeting the estimated 260,000 tonnes of food wasted annually by Irish food businesses as its primary opportunity.

Community subscription (for people experiencing homelessness)

REFEED+ keeps access free for people who need it most — delivered through sponsor-funded subscriptions and trusted local partners.

  • Sponsor-funded subscriptions: Local businesses or community groups can sponsor monthly subscriptions that cover access for individuals.
  • NGO & partner registration: Charities and social services can register beneficiaries and issue in-store vouchers or ID tokens.
  • In-store access: No smartphone required — beneficiaries can redeem a voucher/code at participating stores or be served through partner-led collection points.

Access options

  1. Partner NGOs register individuals and provide a simple voucher or code.
  2. Individuals present the voucher at participating stores and receive designated surplus items.
  3. Alternatively, sponsored community subscriptions can be allocated monthly by local hubs.

All access routes prioritise dignity and privacy — no public app profiles or exposure.

Strategic growth drivers

  • Local cluster expansion: Grow neighbourhood-by-neighbourhood for consistent access.
  • Operational integration: Make donation frictionless through POS integration to increase retention.
  • B2B engagement: Monthly impact reports, gamification and KPIs to keep managers invested.
  • National roadmap: Position REFEED+ for city and national contracts with NGOs and authorities.
