Our Service

A simple in-store donation flow built around existing POS systems

ReFeed+ is a POS-integrated surplus-food donation system for food businesses that turns unsold food into visible, local support.

Staff can select eligible surplus items directly through the store's existing workflow and add them to a donation database, which is then shown through an in-store kiosk for approved recipients.

This can include near-expiry products, post best-before goods, and end-of-day bakery stock that cannot be sold the next day. The system is designed to work for underprivileged individuals, including people who may not have access to a phone.

Our Aims

Designed to reduce waste, widen access, and stay practical for busy staff

The model is built around five priorities that keep the service useful for businesses and accessible for the people who need it.

  • Reduce food waste at the source by making donation as easy as discarding.
  • Improve access across Dublin by turning many stores into micro-distribution points.
  • Remove digital barriers so the system still works for people without smartphones.
  • Maintain fairness through eligibility checks and weekly item limits.
  • Keep the workflow simple so staff will actually use it during busy hours.

How the service would work in practice

01

Surplus is recorded

Staff identify eligible food that cannot be sold and add it into the donation flow through the POS.

02

Food becomes visible

Available items appear at the store access point, making the offer clear without requiring an app.

03

Eligibility is checked

The system verifies access rules and weekly limits so distribution stays fair and controlled.

04

Food is handed over

Approved users receive food locally, turning operational waste into immediate neighbourhood support.
